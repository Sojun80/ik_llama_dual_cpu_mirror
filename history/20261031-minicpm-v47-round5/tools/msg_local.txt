r5: optional socket-local fused MoE step (IK_R5_FMOE_LOCAL)

Each NUMA node runs up/gate, down-input quantization and down rows of its
own half of the selected experts (and half of the shared expert up/gate rows)
with node-local barriers, leaving one cross-node barrier per MoE layer.
Bit-exact; measured ~1% slower than the default path, so off by default.
