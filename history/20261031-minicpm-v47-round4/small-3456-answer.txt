### 1. Storage Subsystem Failure (Critical)
**What happened:**
The system experienced a critical I/O error on the storage device `sdb`, leading to data read failures. This appears to be a hardware or driver-level failure affecting the filesystem `sdb1`.

**Evidence:**
*   **L301:** `kernel: blk_update_request: I/O error, dev sdb, sector 118400`
*   **L302:** `kernel: EXT4-fs warning (device sdb1): I/O error reading directory`
*   **L303:** `storage-agent[422]: read failed for /srv/data`

**Analysis:**
The sequence of logs indicates a cascade failure. First, the kernel detected a low-level block I/O error on sector 118400 of device `sdb` (L301). Shortly after, the EXT4 filesystem wrapper reported an inability to read a directory from `sdb1` (L302). Finally, the application-level `storage-agent` confirmed a read failure for the `/srv/data` mount point (L303). This suggests the storage device `sdb` is becoming unreliable or has failed partially.

**Analysis:**
